# Goals

## Purpose

This document defines the primary goals of Blueprint.

The goals described here represent the outcomes the product is expected to achieve for its users and should guide product decisions throughout the lifetime of the project.

Every new feature should contribute directly to one or more of these goals.

# Primary Goals

## Centralise Project Communication

Provide a single platform where architects and clients can collaborate throughout the lifecycle of an architectural project.

Blueprint should become the primary location for project discussions, decisions, revisions and approvals.

---

## Improve Revision Traceability

Every project revision should have a complete and understandable history.

Users should always be able to identify:

- what changed
- when it changed
- why it changed
- who requested the change
- who approved the change

---

## Simplify Client Collaboration

Enable clients to participate in architectural projects without requiring technical knowledge of CAD or BIM software.

The platform should present information in a clear, accessible and intuitive way.

---

## Reduce Administrative Overhead

Reduce the amount of time architects spend on repetitive administrative tasks such as:

- searching previous conversations
- explaining design revisions
- tracking client feedback
- confirming approvals
- organising project documentation

Architects should spend more time designing and less time managing communication.

---

## Increase Project Transparency

Provide a complete audit trail for every significant project decision.

Both architects and clients should have confidence that project information is accurate, current and easily accessible.

---

## Preserve Project Knowledge

Ensure that valuable project knowledge is retained throughout the entire lifecycle of the project.

No important decision should depend solely on an individual's memory or personal communication history.

---

## Establish a Scalable Collaboration Platform

Build a platform capable of supporting projects of different sizes while maintaining a consistent collaboration experience.

The architecture should allow new capabilities to be introduced without compromising simplicity or maintainability.

---

# Product Goals

Blueprint should:

- provide a clear history of every project revision
- organise communication around project revisions rather than disconnected conversations
- encourage structured feedback instead of fragmented messaging
- simplify approvals and decision tracking
- improve confidence for both architects and clients
- become the authoritative source of project communication

---

# Engineering Goals

The product should be developed with a strong emphasis on quality and long-term maintainability.

Engineering goals include:

- maintain a clean and modular architecture
- prioritise readability over unnecessary complexity
- favour convention over configuration where appropriate
- ensure the platform remains easy to extend
- maintain a high standard of documentation
- automate testing and deployment wherever practical

---

# Long-Term Goals

Over time, Blueprint should evolve into a comprehensive collaboration platform for the architecture industry.

Future capabilities may include:

- intelligent revision comparison
- AI-assisted project summaries
- semantic search across project history
- advanced reporting and analytics
- integrations with industry-standard BIM and CAD software
- additional collaboration workflows for multidisciplinary teams

These capabilities should enhance the core collaboration experience without changing the primary purpose of the platform.

---

# Success Criteria

Blueprint will be considered successful when:

- architects rely on the platform as the primary source of project communication
- clients can easily understand project progress and revisions
- project decisions are fully traceable
- communication becomes more structured and less fragmented
- administrative work is significantly reduced
- project knowledge remains accessible throughout the entire project lifecycle
