# Non Goals

## Purpose

This document defines what Blueprint is intentionally **not** designed to do.

Clearly identifying non-goals helps maintain focus during product development, prevents unnecessary feature creep, and ensures that every engineering effort contributes to the product vision.

A feature request that falls within one of these non-goals should be carefully evaluated before being considered for implementation.

# Why Define Non Goals?

Successful products are defined not only by the problems they solve, but also by the problems they intentionally choose **not** to solve.

Blueprint aims to become an outstanding collaboration platform for architectural projects.

Attempting to solve every problem faced by architects would dilute the product, increase complexity, and reduce its overall value.

The following areas are explicitly outside the scope of Blueprint unless a future strategic decision states otherwise.

---

# Non Goals

## Blueprint Is Not CAD Software

Blueprint will not provide tools for creating or editing architectural drawings.

Professional design work should continue to be performed using dedicated CAD and BIM applications.

Blueprint complements these tools rather than replacing them.

---

## Blueprint Is Not BIM Software

Blueprint is not intended to compete with BIM authoring platforms.

It does not aim to replace software used for modelling buildings, managing building information or producing construction documentation.

Blueprint focuses on collaboration surrounding architectural projects.

---

## Blueprint Is Not a Rendering Platform

The platform will not generate photorealistic renders, animations or visualisations.

Any rendering capabilities should remain the responsibility of specialised software.

---

## Blueprint Is Not General Project Management Software

Blueprint is not intended to replace project management platforms.

Managing budgets, schedules, invoices, procurement, staffing and general business operations falls outside the scope of the product.

Project collaboration should remain centred around architectural revisions and communication.

---

## Blueprint Is Not a File Storage Service

Although project files will be stored by the platform, Blueprint is not intended to become a replacement for general-purpose cloud storage providers.

File storage exists to support collaboration, not as an end in itself.

---

## Blueprint Is Not a Messaging Application

Blueprint is not intended to replace email or instant messaging platforms.

Communication should remain structured around revisions, comments and project decisions rather than real-time conversations.

---

## Blueprint Is Not an AI Design Tool

Artificial Intelligence should assist architects by improving access to information and reducing repetitive work.

Blueprint will not attempt to generate architectural designs or replace professional judgement.

Architectural decisions should always remain under human control.

---

## Blueprint Is Not a Document Management System

Blueprint is not intended to become a generic repository for every document related to a construction project.

Only documents that contribute to collaboration and project understanding should be managed within the platform.

---

## Blueprint Is Not a Construction Management Platform

Managing contractors, construction progress, procurement, inspections and site activities is outside the intended scope of Blueprint.

The platform focuses on the design and client collaboration phases of a project.

---

# Product Boundaries

When evaluating a new feature, the following questions should be considered:

- Does this improve collaboration between architects and clients?
- Does this make project communication clearer or more traceable?
- Does this reduce administrative work?
- Does this strengthen the project's single source of truth?

If the answer to these questions is **no**, the feature is unlikely to align with Blueprint's purpose.

---

# Future Considerations

Some capabilities currently listed as non-goals may become relevant in the future through integrations rather than native implementation.

Whenever possible, Blueprint should integrate with specialised tools instead of attempting to replace them.

This approach allows each platform to focus on its core strengths while providing users with a cohesive workflow.

---

# Summary

Blueprint is intentionally focused.

Its objective is not to become an all-in-one architecture platform.

Its objective is to become the best platform for collaboration, communication, revision tracking and decision management between architects and their clients.

Maintaining this focus is essential to delivering a product that remains simple, valuable and sustainable over the long term.
